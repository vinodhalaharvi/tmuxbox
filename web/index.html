Terminal bean

type
turn to the pane that matches best, by name or contents
Enter
open that pane as a terminal
Shift Enter
with tmuxbox running: open it in a terminal window of your own
Ctrl ]
leave the terminal, back to the bean
Tab, Shift Tab
next and previous pane
Esc
clear what you typed
← → ↑ ↓
turn the bean
⌘ or Ctrl + ↑ ↓
bigger, smaller
⌘ or Ctrl + ← →
shorter, longer
Home
reset shape and view
⌘ or Ctrl + ⌫
forget usage
Alt Enter
ask the browser for full screen
F1
show or hide this

On a touch screen: drag to turn, pinch to resize, tap a pane to open it, tap the text to type.
